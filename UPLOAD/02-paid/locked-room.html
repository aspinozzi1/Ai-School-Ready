question | correct answer | wrong | wrong | wrong | idea | level | why

      
idea is the concept the lock tests. The game tracks it, brings a jammed
      lock's idea back for you to clear, and never shows two locks on the same idea side by side, so use only
      three or four ideas across the room. level is 1 simple, 2 sturdy, 3 stiff — you pick your own
      difficulty by picking the lock, so you need some of each. why is the one line shown after a wrong
      answer, and it is the part that does the teaching.
