## (d) Format — and a problem this niche has that we must design around

**The dominant expectation in this niche is EDITABLE.** Every comparable
advertises typing your own dates: "fully editable", "edit on Google Slides and
PowerPoint", "edit the entire template on Canva". We ship flat PDF.

**That is the fifth independent signal for the editable pipeline**, after
weekly newsletter template editable (1,216), classroom procedures powerpoint
(727), newsletter template editable (704) and special education morning
meeting slides (365). The engineering week keeps being the highest-leverage
unbuilt thing we have.

**So this product cannot win on the sign-up sheet itself, and should not try.**
A print-and-write sign-up sheet is genuinely useful on a clipboard at Back to
School Night — but a buyer comparing it to an editable one will pick the
editable one, and they would be right to.

**Win on the part nobody sells: what to actually say in twenty-five
conferences.** That is the real dread, it is squarely our AI Growth Eval
territory, and it needs no editable file at all.

**Contents (~12 pages, $8):**
1. Cover and how to use
2. Sign-up sheet — parents mark their top three times (the comparables' best
   idea, and a genuinely better design than first-come-first-served)
3. In-person or virtual column, since that is now standard
4. Master schedule grid for the teacher
5. Reminder slip to send home
6. **Conference prep sheet** — one page per student, filled in beforehand
7. **The talking-points page** — the printable, zero-technology half: a
   strengths / growth / one ask structure with sentence starters
8. **AI conference prep page** — paste de-identified scores and patterns
   ("a 4th grader, 6/10 on multi-digit multiplication, strong on estimation")
   and get back three talking points and one specific ask for home. **Never a
   name, never a roster** — the same rule as every data product we ship.
9. Notes page for during the conference
10. Follow-up log — what was agreed, what happens next
11. No-show follow-up note
12. How this is built, and what it is not

**Guardrail, consistent with the IEP line:** the AI half works on
de-identified data only. It drafts talking points a teacher then edits; it
never characterises a child, never suggests a diagnosis, and never drafts
anything that reads as a formal determination.
