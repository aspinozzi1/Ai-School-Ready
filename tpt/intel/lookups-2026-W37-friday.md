## This list asks a different question than previous Friday lists

Friday's lane was designed to enter **crowded, proven** markets and win
on the AI Growth Eval. Previous Friday lists therefore asked which
format family has the most durable demand and the highest price ceiling.

**That question is now the wrong one**, because of what your traffic
screen showed on Sunday: roughly 31 search visits in 30 days. An
unranked store does not surface on a crowded shelf — so on a 40K–75K
shelf the Growth Eval never gets seen, and a differentiator nobody sees
does no work. The CVC binder shipped 09-04 against exactly such a shelf.

**The fix keeps the lane and changes its door:**

> Build thick products in proven format families, but **title and tag
> them on a thin-shelf long-tail** instead of the fat head phrase.

Same build, same page count, same $15–20 price band. Only the naming
changes. The head phrase becomes a tag — which is already our standing
rule everywhere else (`classroom rules`, `special education`,
`back to school`) and simply never got applied to this lane.

So this list hunts **artifact names, not format names.** Every format
word we hold is unenterable:

| Phrase | Vol | Shelf | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| morning work | 70,345 | >500K | 117 |
| sight words | 15,043 | 150K–300K | 100 |
| cvc words worksheets | 7,473 | 40K–75K | 130 |
| special education task boxes | 1,456 | 20K–40K | 49 |
| reading intervention binder | 255 | 3K–8K | 46 |

The theory: **format words are crowded, artifact words may not be.**
That is precisely how `iep at a glance` (4,600) turned up hiding under a
fat `special education` head term.

**If nothing below comes back thin, that is a real answer** — it would
mean the compete lane should pause until the store has rank, with its
build capacity moved to the win lanes. Your call, and worth making
deliberately rather than drifting into.

## ⚠️ Trademark check before any of these becomes a title

Two phrases in this space are **registered trademarks held by living
authors who actively enforce them**:

- **Social Stories™** — Carol Gray
- **Zones of Regulation®** — Leah Kuypers / Social Thinking

TPT's own dashboard warns sellers about trademarked search terms. Even
if one returns a beautiful number, **we do not title, subtitle or tag on
it.** The generic alternative is `social narratives`, which is #10 below
and is why it is on the list. Same posture as the UFLI deferral: a great
score is not a licence.

Please verify current trademark status before we use either word
anywhere — I am flagging a risk, not delivering a legal opinion.
