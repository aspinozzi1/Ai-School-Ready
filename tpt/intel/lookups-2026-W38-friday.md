## A note on how I would read a weak result here

If Priority 1 comes back thin on *demand* rather than thin on *competition* —
small numbers everywhere, like `vibe coding` — that is not automatically fatal
for this lane, because you have already decided to enter one category ahead of
its search volume and the reasoning applies equally here.

But it would mean **two lanes both betting on the future at once**, which is a
materially different risk than one. Worth knowing before we build rather than
after, which is the only reason I am flagging it now.
