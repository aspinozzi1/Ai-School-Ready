## What this list has to decide, and it is bigger than one Wednesday

**Whether Lane C can stand on its own phrases at all.** Priority 1 is four rows
and they settle it. I am putting a real finding in front of you first, because
it changes what those four rows mean.

### The premise behind "Lane C is the safe lane" has never been tested

The working assumption has been that Lane C is the least exposed of the three
because it sells on established PD phrases rather than on the `vibe coding`
category bet. **Our own dataset does not support that**, and I should have
flagged it earlier. The keywords worksheet already records, from measured pulls:

> "...versus 733 for 'artificial intelligence' and **effectively nothing for
> teacher-PD phrasing**."

So the one adjacent measurement we have says the PD family is empty on TPT.
**Lane C may be no safer than Lane A — it may just be untested.** The four rows
at the top are what turn that from a worry into a fact.

### Meanwhile the demand outside TPT is real, and that is the trap

Among districts that the Center for Reinventing Public Education counts as AI
early adopters, **86 percent offered teacher professional development in 2025-26,
up from 63 percent the year before**, and the early-adopter cohort itself grew
from **40 districts to 79**. Guidance about AI use went from 65 to 78 percent.

**Read that carefully, because it is easy to read backwards.** It describes
79 self-selected leading districts, not the market. And more to the point:
**those districts buy through procurement** — contracts, discretionary grant
funds, named vendors — **not from a TPT listing.** A real and growing demand
that is mostly not reachable from where we sell is not an opportunity for this
store; it is a reason to be precise about who the TPT buyer for a PD kit
actually is. **That buyer is one teacher-leader or coach with a small building
budget**, and there are far fewer of them than the headlines suggest.

**None of this reopens the lane.** You restored it by directive on 09-20 and
that is settled. It changes how the products should be *named*, which is what
Priority 3 is about.
