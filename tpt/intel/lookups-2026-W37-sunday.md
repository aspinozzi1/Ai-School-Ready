## Why this list is not the one the Wednesday pulse predicted

The 09-02 pulse said Friday's list would lead with the Week-3 calendar
theme, "Newsletters and families" — newsletter, classroom newsletter,
open house, translation for families, family FAQ, positive note.

It does not, and the reason is the concentration rule. Sunday's headline
build is the IEP Goal and Progress Monitoring Tracker, which validated
at the second and third best win scores in our whole dataset. The rule
says a second product in a family we already hold beats a first in a new
one. Opening a newsletter family the same week we plant a flag in
special education data would split the drop across two families and
give the tracker no supporting frees to funnel from.

So lane 1 leads with phrases that feed the tracker, and the newsletter
theme keeps one test slot at number 9. If it comes back with a big
number and a Strong badge, it moves up next week on its own merit
rather than because a calendar row said so. The ranking outranks the
calendar — that is already the standing rule, this is just the first
week it actually bites.

| 1 | **iep data collection sheets** | The most important one on this list. Our validated phrase is "progress monitoring tracking sheet" (4,174 · 3K–8K · Strong), but "data collection" is the wording the special education world actually uses day to day — every practitioner blog and every competing pack is titled that way. If it scores well, it becomes the lead free that funnels into the $9 tracker, and it may belong in the paid title too. |
| 2 | **iep binder** | Whether Sunday's paid product is framed as a *binder* rather than a sheets pack. | 4 | **behavior tracking sheet** | Two independent practitioner sources name frequency and behavior sheets as the most-used data sheet type of all. Decides whether a behavior data free ships alongside the academic one — and whether behavior is a whole second product rather than a page. |
| 5 | **homeschool record keeping** | Lane 2's second door, and overdue. "homeschool planner" (2,415 · 20K–40K · Promising) scores **81** — near the bottom of our entire table — and it is the only homeschool phrase we have ever validated. Record keeping is a legal obligation in many states, which is exactly the kind of must-do search that converts. If this comes back weak too, lane 2 needs a rethink, not another planner re-cut. |
