### The trap in the CRPE numbers, stated so nobody reads them backwards

They describe **79 self-selected leading districts, not the market.** And those
districts buy through **procurement** — contracts, discretionary grant funds,
named vendors — **not from a TPT listing.** A real and growing demand that is
largely unreachable from where this store sells is not an opportunity; it is a
reason to be exact about who the TPT buyer for a PD kit actually is. **That buyer
is one teacher-leader or coach with a small building budget**, and there are far
fewer of them than the headlines imply.

**Which makes an old unanswered question urgent:** has Kit 1 or Kit 2 ever sold?
Asked four times since 09-16. It is now the only direct evidence available on
whether a PD kit sells on TPT at all, and this week's entire worksheet is about
that question.
