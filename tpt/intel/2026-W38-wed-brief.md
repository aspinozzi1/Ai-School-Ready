## (d) Contents — IEP Organization Binder (~14 pages, $10)

The job: turn a caseload of scattered paper into one binder a case manager can
hand to a substitute, carry to a meeting, and find anything in.

1. Cover and how to use
2. **Binder cover** — editable-by-hand title block, year, case manager
3. **Spine label** — two widths, because binders vary
4. **Eight section dividers** — Students · Goals · Data · Meetings · Services ·
   Communication · Accommodations · Notes
5. **Caseload overview** — one row per student, **student numbers not names**
6. **Meeting dates tracker** — annual review and re-evaluation due dates
7. **Contact log** — who was spoken to, when, about what
8. **"What is in this binder" index** — the page a substitute reads first
9. **Confidentiality page** — the same handling guidance as IEP at a Glance,
   because this binder holds more than any single sheet in it
10. What this is not

**Guardrail, and it is stronger here than on any product we have shipped.**
A completed version of this binder is a caseload of confidential records in one
place. The confidentiality page is not optional and it goes near the front, not
the back. **The caseload overview uses student numbers only**, with the key kept
separately — the same rule as the at-a-glance sheet, and the reason is the same:
the moment names go in, this becomes a document that cannot leave a locked room.

**No AI page on this product.** It is an organisational shell; there is no data
to synthesise and adding a prompt for the sake of the brand signature would be
padding. The AI work lives in the products it organises. Saying so plainly in
the listing is more honest than a token page.
