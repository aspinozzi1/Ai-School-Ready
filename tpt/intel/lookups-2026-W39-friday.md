## What the web said this week, and what it is worth

**Genius hour is alive in 2026 and has moved toward exactly what we make.**
Current program descriptions for passion projects list AI, coding, game
development and app development as the skill set, and there is a Genius Hour
Academy running teacher training in July 2026. That supports rows 1 and 2 being
the right door. **It does not measure them** — the `service minute tracker`
lesson stands, and a practitioner blog naming a thing is not evidence anyone
searches for it on TPT.

**Our design law got corroborated from outside, which is worth knowing.**
Teaching Entrepreneurship's 2026 AI lesson plan lands on the same rule this
lane already runs on: generative AI belongs in an entrepreneurship class *after*
the student has something to think with, and **the student keeps the problem
choice, the customer evidence and the judgment.** That is our "students do the
research, the specification, the testing and the pitch; the grown-up holds the
keyboard," arrived at independently. It is a line worth putting in the listing,
because it answers the objection that we are letting AI do the thinking.

**And the lane has a competitor set we have never priced in.** No-code app
builders aimed at classrooms are a real 2026 category — Thunkable, Adalo, Appy
Pie and others. The honest comparison, and it is a good one for us:

- **Thunkable's free tier lets students build but not publish.** Publishing
  starts at the **Builder plan, about $59 a month**; the tiers above run to
  about $189 a month, and education pricing is quote-only through a sales
  application.
- **Every student needs an account**, which is the thing our lane refuses on
  principle and the reason the teacher holds the keyboard.

So the Lane B pitch is not "this is easier than Thunkable." It is: **your class
ships a real app, nobody signs up for anything, nothing costs a monthly fee,
and the file belongs to the school when the unit ends.** Say it that way and it
is true; say it as "better than no-code tools" and a buyer will correct us in a
review.
