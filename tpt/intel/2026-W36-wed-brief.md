## Format, tags, and the binding design laws

- **Tag blanket for every lane-4 listing** (all long-tails are <1K–3K
  resources, so this is free reach): deaf and hard of hearing, teacher
  of the deaf, DHH itinerant, iep goals deaf and hard of hearing, asl
  back to school, self advocacy, executive functioning, special
  education.
- **September is Deaf Awareness Month** — a legitimate seasonal rider
  for lane-4 tags and pin copy this month.
- **Provenance line, every lane-4 product and listing, verbatim:**
  "Built and audited by two certified teachers — and the parents of a
  deaf child." That sentence is the entire disclosure. No age, school,
  device, communication mode, or any other identifying family detail
  appears anywhere — product, listing, pin, or preview.
- **Pedagogy never audiology.** Teaching supports only; the legal note
  reads "informational; follow your audiologist's and IEP team's
  guidance."
- **Diversity is not optional**: DHH students and families use ASL,
  listening-and-spoken-language, bimodal approaches, devices or none.
  Never write as though one path is the path. No outdated terminology.
- **Lane 3 design law**: the grown-up holds the keyboard — chatbots are
  13+, AI coaches the adult, kids think and decide, child identity never
  enters a prompt.
- Custom category "Deaf Education"; lane-4 pins get the "Deaf Education"
  board.
- **Never** in a lane-3 title: "business" (753 vs 75K–150K resources) or
  "marketing" (Challenging).
