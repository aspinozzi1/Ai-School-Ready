## Why the Deaf Awareness Month pack is FREE

Two reasons, and the second is the real one.

274 searches is modest, so the revenue at any price is small either way.

More importantly, **the store's binding constraint is reviews and
downloads, not revenue.** A free product on a sub-1K shelf, in season,
in a niche where we have genuine standing, is the best
review-generating opportunity currently available to us. Frees get
downloaded far more than paid items sell, and reviews are one of the few
ranking signals a store at zero can actually earn.

## (b) The borrowed build, and why ABC

With lane 3 out, Wednesday needs a second product. `abc data sheet` —
**2,152 · 1K–3K · Strong, score 1,076** — is the highest-scoring
validated target we hold that nothing serves yet, and it is thin-shelf.

ABC (antecedent, behavior, consequence) is the standard structure for
recording an incident before a behavior plan is written. It cross-sells
directly with Sunday's Free Behavior Tracker and the IEP data products.

**Guardrail, same as the at-a-glance sheet:** behavior records describe a
named child's conduct. The AI page works on de-identified patterns only,
never diagnoses, never names a disorder, and never drafts a behavior plan
as though it were the team's decision. We record and summarize; the team
decides.

## (d) Format, contents and the lane-4 design laws

### Deaf Awareness Month Classroom Pack (~8–10 pages, free)

Audience: **general-education teachers with a DHH student**, which is
this line's stated thesis. Not a Deaf studies curriculum.

Contents:
1. What Deaf Awareness Month is — short, accurate, sourced
2. **Ways people communicate** — ASL, listening and spoken language,
   bimodal, cochlear implants, hearing aids, no devices — presented as
   equally valid paths, never a hierarchy
3. Communication-repair strategies poster (rephrase, don't just repeat)
4. Classroom-access quick wins: face the student, one speaker at a time,
   captions on, check the acoustics
5. Fingerspelling alphabet page (`asl posters` 274 · Promising — tag it)
6. **Speech banana page** — what the audiogram region means *for what a
   teacher says in class*, never interpretation of any child's results
7. A short "ask, don't assume" page for classmates' questions
8. Where to learn more — **Deaf-authored sources, credited**

**Binding, from `deaf-ed-niche.md`:**
- **Pedagogy, never audiology.** The speech banana page is teaching
  implications only. Legal note on every page set: "informational;
  follow your audiologist's and IEP team's guidance."
- **Accuracy and respect.** No path treated as correct. No outdated
  terms. Deaf culture treated with respect.
- **We do not author Deaf history or Deaf culture ourselves.** `deaf
  history` scored 2 and is not a target, but the deeper reason is that
  this is Deaf community material and a hearing-authored version would
  be presumptuous. **Point to Deaf-authored resources and credit them.**
- **Provenance line, exactly as approved:** "Built and audited by two
  certified teachers — and the parents of a deaf child." Nothing further
  about the owner's family — no age, school, device or communication
  mode — appears anywhere.
- **Privacy doubled.** DHH students are a small, identifiable
  population; the describe-the-learner-never-the-child rule is repeated
  in this product.
