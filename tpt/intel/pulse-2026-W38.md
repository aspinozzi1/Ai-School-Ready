### A format problem this niche has, and how the build answers it

Every comparable conference product advertises being **editable** — type your
own dates, edit in Slides, PowerPoint or Canva. We ship flat PDF. A buyer
comparing our sign-up sheet against an editable one picks the editable one,
and is right to.

**So the build does not try to win on the sign-up sheet.** It wins on the part
nobody sells: what to actually say across twenty-five conferences. A prep
sheet, a printable talking-points structure with sentence starters, and an AI
page that turns de-identified scores into three talking points and one ask for
home. No editable file needed, and it is squarely our own differentiator.

**This is the fifth independent signal for the editable pipeline** — after
weekly newsletter template editable (1,216), classroom procedures powerpoint
(727), newsletter template editable (704) and special education morning meeting
slides (365). That engineering week keeps being the highest-leverage unbuilt
thing in the queue.

### Pricing

$8 for ~12 pages, licence $4. The niche runs from free single sheets to a
$24 editable bundle, so this sits sensibly between — more than a free sheet,
well under the bundle. (Prices from search snippets; TPT 403s automated
fetches.)
