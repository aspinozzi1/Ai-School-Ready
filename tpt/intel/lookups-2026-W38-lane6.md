## If `vibe coding` comes back empty

**That is not a reason to drop the line.** It most likely means the
audience does not yet have a shared word for what they want — which is
what being early actually looks like from the inside.

In that case: **title on the artifact, not the method.** Lead with
"self-checking activities" or "teacher made games," keep `vibe coding`
in the tags and the description, and let the phrase grow into us. If it
becomes the word, our listings already carry it.

The reverse is also worth watching: if #1 returns real volume with a
Strong or Promising badge on a thin shelf, that is the best opening this
store has ever been handed, and the line should take more than one
Sunday slot.
