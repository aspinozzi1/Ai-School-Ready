## Read this first: AI teacher training is now a legal requirement in a growing number of states

This is the strongest commercial finding in weeks and it is not a keyword.

- **Maryland's SB 720, the AI Ready Schools Act,** requires the state
  department of education to provide AI teacher training **by 1 July 2027**,
  designate an AI coordinator in every local school system, and publish AI
  guidance and a **tool-evaluation rubric**.
- **Virginia and Ohio** passed 2026 legislation requiring AI training covering
  classroom use, data-privacy obligations and supervision of AI-assisted
  instruction.
- **More than 70 AI-in-education bills across 27 states in 2026.**
- Teachers **must be compensated** for the training and **cannot be charged**
  to attend — so the money comes from a district budget, not a teacher's
  pocket.
- And the line that matters most: **most districts remain underprepared to
  deliver the training themselves.**

**A legal mandate, a deadline, a budget, and nobody ready to meet it.** Lane C
is the only lane in this store pointed at that, and Kit 1 and Kit 2 already
sit in the family. This is worth more than any single keyword below, and the
phrases in Priority 1 are chosen to find the door into it.

**This is web research, not a TPT row.** It tells us the demand is real and
funded. It does not tell us anyone types these words into TPT, which is what
rows 1 to 5 are for.

## A correction I owe you on Sunday's game

When I recommended the review game I said it was "partly served" by Blooket
and Gimkit. That was incomplete. **There is now a category of AI game
generators aimed at teachers** — Rosebud AI's education lab, Eduaide's game
builder, and others — that produce classroom games from a plain-language
prompt with no coding.

The differentiator still holds, and it is the same one as page 3 already
argues: **those are games on somebody's platform, behind an account, hosted by
them.** Ours is a file you own, that runs with the wifi down, that no
subscription or district block can take away, and whose every rule you can
change. That is a real and defensible difference.

But **"you cannot do this anywhere else" is weaker than I implied when I
pitched it**, and I would rather say so now than have a buyer say it in a
review. Rows 11 to 13 size that market properly.
