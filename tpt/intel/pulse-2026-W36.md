### Context meter (WebSearch, TPT browse)

The routines/procedures niche's winners are **editable slide decks with
matching posters** — the category leader is an editable slides pack
(9,600+ teachers) with 70+ slides plus 70+ posters and 25+ daily-routine
slides; another runs 160+ slides. Year-long visual bundles sell around
**$93** (heavily discounted from ~$144); standalone full versions around
**$5**.

**Pricing read for Week 2:** the niche's *standalone* items sit low (~$5)
while the money is in giant editable bundles we are not built to make.
Our $8 Sub-Plan Builder must therefore justify its price by being
something the $5 poster packs are not — an **AI-drafting system**, not a
poster set. Keep the price at $8 (already lowered from $10) and lead the
title with what they can't get for $5.

**Format read:** this niche rewards *editable* and *poster/slide* formats.
Our print-and-go PDFs are a weaker fit for the routines phrase itself —
so target routines wording in the FREE items (posters/checklists) and let
the paid item lead on sub plans + AI, where our format is native.
