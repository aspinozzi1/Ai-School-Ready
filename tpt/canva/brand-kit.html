Type rules

✔  Fredoka for display only — it lives on rendered art, not deck text boxes.

✔  Decks ship as editable PPTX, so deck body type stays a universal font.

✔  Luckiest Guy only on layer-1 pins, outlined, in caps.

✘  Handwriting / script faces are decorative only — never instructions. Stage directions stay italic sans.

✘  Never letter-space body text; save tracking for uppercase kickers.

Bright Scholar · 03
The Intern
Flat fills · one heavy ink outline
friendly but not babyish
Who he is
The kits teach that AI is a first-year intern — drafts fast, doesn't know your students, never gets the final say. The mascot IS that character. The AI sparkle antenna says what he is; the pencil badge says what he does: the intern drafts, the teacher decides.
Where he appears
✔  Every kit cover (bottom-right) · TPT covers · pins · the deck title slide
Never
✘  Inside worksheet bodies — he brands the product, he doesn't decorate the content.  ✘  Never recolour him, outline him in anything but ink, or add accessories.
Bright Scholar · 04
The Three-Layer Loudness Rule
1 · PINS & SOCIAL
Loudest. Saturated field, outlined display type, doodles, fanned real product pages, page-count badge, CTA pill.
2 · Covers & listings
Middle. Ink ground, Fredoka titles, sunny badge, rainbow bar, the intern bottom-right.
3 · Kit interiors
Professional + warm. Sunny h2 chips, rounded cards, blue slide pills, cream page. Board-meeting safe.
Hard rules
✔  Admin one-pager and References stay at the quiet end of layer 3.
✔  Legal blocks are never trimmed to fit a page; marketing copy is.
✔  Every product cover carries the rainbow bar and the intern.
✘  Never mix layer voices: no doodles on interiors, no quiet covers.
The One Hard Rule slab
Always ink-on-navy with the sunny border — the loudest object in any interior document, everywhere the same.
