==================================================================
EASEL SETUP — SELF-ADVOCACY WORKSHEETS FOR DEAF & HARD OF HEARING
STUDENTS (DHH SELF-ADVOCACY PACK)
About 10 minutes, done once, inside TPT's own editor.
Result: the "Easel Activity included" badge + a digitally assignable
version of the full pack. Same two tools as always: ANSWER BOX for
anything the student types; TEXT/page directions for the instructions.
==================================================================

HOW TO START
1. Open the live listing on TPT (logged in as the seller).
2. In the listing's "Easel" section choose "Create Easel Activity"
   (the product PDF imports automatically, one Easel page per PDF page).
3. Work the pages below, then save/publish.

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 1 · "I need…" sentence starters
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over every one of the 8 fill-in-the-blank lines (noisy
room / spoken-only directions / can't see face or lips / device or
support not working / video with no captions / missed a classmate's
comment / fire drill or loud alarm / unfamiliar substitute).
Page instructions:
"Fill in each blank with what actually helps you. There's no wrong
answer — this is about your own words, not what's 'supposed to'
happen."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 2 · Weekly self-check + script builder
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over:
- all 6 self-check checkboxes ("This week: check what's true for you")
- the open notes line ("Something to bring up at my next check-in")
- all 4 write-in lines/blanks in the "Build your script: meeting a
  new teacher" section
Page instructions:
"Check what's true for you this week — it's not your IEP, just a
quick way to notice what's working and what to bring up at your next
check-in. Then build your new-teacher script: fill in your own words
for how you communicate and what helps."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 3 · Practice scenarios
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over the write-in line under each of the 4 scenario
cards (group work, fire drill/loud alarm, video with no captions,
unfamiliar substitute).
Page instructions:
"Read each scenario with your ToD or a family member. Practice saying
what you'd do out loud, then write it in the space below."

------------------------------------------------------------------
AFTER PUBLISHING THE EASEL ACTIVITY
------------------------------------------------------------------
Add this line to the END of the first paragraph of the listing
description (edit listing > Description):

"Includes a ready-to-assign digital Easel version — students can
complete the whole pack online, no prep."

Do NOT add that line before the Easel Activity is live.
