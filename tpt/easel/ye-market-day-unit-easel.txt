==================================================================
EASEL SETUP — ENTREPRENEURSHIP UNIT WITH AI (START A BUSINESS UNIT)
About 8 minutes, done once, inside TPT's own editor.
Result: the "Easel Activity included" badge on page 4 of the unit —
the one page every kid in the group fills out by hand.
==================================================================

HOW TO START
1. Open the live listing on TPT (logged in as the seller).
2. In the listing's "Easel" section choose "Create Easel Activity"
   (the product PDF imports automatically, one Easel page per PDF page).
3. Work ONLY page 4 below (the rest of the unit is teacher-run — leave
   pages 1-3 and 5-7 as plain PDF pages, no answer boxes needed), then
   save/publish.

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 4 · "My Role & My Price" (the kid worksheet)
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over:
1. My business or team name — one line
2. My job on market day — four checkboxes (Cashier & Money Counter /
   Greeter & Helper / Maker & Restocker / Sign Artist & Announcer)
3. What our team is selling or making — one line
4. My price, using the Rule of Three — the "materials cost x 3 ="
   fill-in-the-blank line
5. One sentence I'll say to a customer — one line
6. Before market day — three checkboxes (practiced pitch, sign
   finished, knows the price and can explain it)

Page instructions:
"Fill this out after your group finishes Days 1-3. Pick the job you
want for market day, write your team's price using the Rule of Three,
and practice your one sentence out loud before you check the last
three boxes."

------------------------------------------------------------------
AFTER PUBLISHING THE EASEL ACTIVITY
------------------------------------------------------------------
Add this line to the END of the first paragraph of the listing
description (edit listing > Description):

"Includes a ready-to-assign digital Easel version of the kid
worksheet (page 4) — students can fill in their role and price
online, no prep."

Do NOT add that line before the Easel Activity is live.
