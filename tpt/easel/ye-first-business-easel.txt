==================================================================
EASEL SETUP — MY FIRST BUSINESS (YOUNG ENTREPRENEUR WORKBOOK, AGES 6-10)
About 10 minutes, done once, inside TPT's own editor.
Result: the listing gets the "Easel Activity included" badge, buyers
can assign the workbook digitally, and students type into it online.
==================================================================

HOW TO START
1. Open the live listing on TPT (you must be logged in as the seller).
2. Find the "Easel" section on the listing management page and choose
   "Create Easel Activity" (TPT imports the product PDF automatically,
   one Easel page per PDF page).
3. Work through the pages below. Two tools do everything:
   - ANSWER BOX = a box a student can type in (place one over every
     blank line or empty box).
   - TEXT (teacher annotation) = instructions students see but can't
     edit (paste the per-page instructions below somewhere clear, or
     put them in the page's directions field if the editor offers one).
4. Save/publish the Easel Activity when all pages are done.

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 1 · My big idea
------------------------------------------------------------------
Place ANSWER BOXES over:
- the line after "My business will make or do:"
- the line after "People will want it because:"
- the line after "My business name is:"
Paste as page instructions:
"Type your answers right on the lines! Big ideas welcome — spelling
doesn't count today. The teal 'coach prompt' box at the bottom is for
your teacher or grown-up, not you."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 2 · What will it cost me? + The Rule of Three
------------------------------------------------------------------
Place ANSWER BOXES over:
- each empty cell of the supply table (3 columns x 4 rows, plus the
  "Total it costs to make ONE" cell)
- the three Rule of Three boxes (My supplies / My work / A little extra)
- the two blanks in "My price: $___  ·  If I sell one, I keep: $___"
Paste as page instructions:
"Fill in the supply table first (ask an adult to help with prices).
Then add the three boxes together — that total is your price!"

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 3 · Tell the world
------------------------------------------------------------------
Place ANSWER BOXES over:
- the big sign-design box (one large box; students can type their sign
  words — or design on paper and describe it here)
- the line after "My selling sentence:"
Paste as page instructions:
"Design your sign in the big box — type the words you'd letter by
hand, BIG. Then write the one sentence you'll say to customers."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 4 · Sale day + What I learned
------------------------------------------------------------------
Place ANSWER BOXES over:
- each empty cell of the sale-day tally table
- the three money blanks (collected / minus supplies / MY PROFIT)
- the three reflection lines (best moment / would change / next idea)
Paste as page instructions:
"After your sale: fill the tally, do the two subtractions, and meet
your PROFIT. Then answer the three questions like a real founder."

------------------------------------------------------------------
AFTER PUBLISHING THE EASEL ACTIVITY
------------------------------------------------------------------
Add this line to the END of the first paragraph of the listing
description (edit listing > Description):

"Includes a ready-to-assign digital Easel version — students can
complete every page online, no prep."

Do NOT add that line before the Easel Activity is live.
