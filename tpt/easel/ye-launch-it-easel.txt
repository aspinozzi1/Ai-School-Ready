==================================================================
EASEL SETUP — LAUNCH IT (YOUNG ENTREPRENEUR KIT, AGES 9-12)
About 12 minutes, done once, inside TPT's own editor.
Result: the "Easel Activity included" badge + a digitally assignable
version of the whole kit. Same two tools as always: ANSWER BOX for
anything students type; TEXT/page directions for the instructions.
==================================================================

HOW TO START
1. Open the live listing on TPT (logged in as the seller).
2. In the listing's "Easel" section choose "Create Easel Activity"
   (the product PDF imports automatically, one Easel page per PDF page).
3. Work the pages below, then save/publish.

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 1 · The one-page business plan
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over every plan line/box:
business name · what I sell/do · who my customer is · the problem I
solve (box) · why me (box) · my price & why it's fair · my launch goal.
Page instructions:
"Fill in your plan in pencil-mindset: you can come back and change any
box as you learn more. The teal coach prompts are for your teacher or
grown-up to run — your job is the thinking."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 2 · Market research
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over:
- the three interview-question lines
- every cell of the interview table (Person / Most interesting thing /
  Would they buy? — 5 rows)
- the line after "What I'm changing because of what I heard:"
Page instructions:
"Write three questions that can't be answered yes/no. Interview five
real people, capture what they said, then record the one change
you're making because of it."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 3 · Price it like a pro
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over:
- the four pricing cells (supplies / my time / grow extra / MY PRICE)
- the three blanks in the sell-10 profit sentence
Page instructions:
"Rule of Three: supplies + your work + a little extra = your price.
Then run the sell-10 math BEFORE launch day — if the profit looks
tiny, change something."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 4 · The pitch
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOX over the big 30-second-pitch box.
Page instructions:
"Write your 30-second pitch: name, what it is, why it's great, the
price, how to get one. Read it out loud twice — anywhere you stumble,
rewrite."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 5 · The ledger + online-safety contract
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over:
- every cell of the ledger table (Date / What happened / Money in /
  Money out / Running total — 7 rows)
- the four profit-split blanks (total / keep / save / reinvest)
- the two signature lines of the contract (entrepreneur / grown-up
  partner) — typing a name counts as signing the digital version.
Page instructions:
"The ledger is where the truth lives — every sale and every supply
run gets a row. Read the online-safety contract together and both
type your names to sign."

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 6 · Debrief
------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER BOXES over the three reflection lines (what surprised me /
hardest moment / version 2.0).
Page instructions:
"Think like a founder: what surprised you, what was hard, and what
version 2.0 looks like. Honest answers beat impressive ones."

------------------------------------------------------------------
AFTER PUBLISHING THE EASEL ACTIVITY
------------------------------------------------------------------
Add this line to the END of the first paragraph of the listing
description (edit listing > Description):

"Includes a ready-to-assign digital Easel version — students can
complete the whole kit online, no prep."

Do NOT add that line before the Easel Activity is live.
