# Upload sheet — launch day

Eight listings. Everything below is paste-ready. Files are in the delivered
`UPLOAD` folder: `01-free/`, `02-paid/`, `03-covers/`.

**Before you list anything:** create the seller account as **Premium** ($59.95/yr, 80%
royalty). Basic is $29 one time at 55% and does not credit toward an upgrade later. On a
$24 kit that is $19.20 versus $12.90 a sale.

**W-9 identity:** your SSN, or the EIN of a business you currently own. **Not** the old
AI-Ready School LLC EIN, which is now a different business.

**Store name:** AI-Ready School

**On every paid listing, set the additional-license price to about half the product price**
($10 on a $24 kit, $4 on an $8 pack, $6 on a $12 pack — never at or above the product price).
TpT defaults to 10% off, which
on a $24 kit is $21.60 and no principal buys twenty of those. Ten dollars is what makes a
whole-staff purchase happen, and a staff purchase is the actual business.
