# AI-Ready School

**Professional development your school runs itself.**

A subscription platform giving schools all-access to a growing library of ready-to-run
Professional Development kits. Each kit is a self-facilitated PD session: the school's
own coach or teacher-leader downloads a facilitator deck, a word-for-word script, and
handouts, and runs the training with staff — no consultants, no recordings.

Built by **Adam & Katelyn Spinozzi** — a husband-and-wife team of certified educators
with over 20 combined years in the classroom. Adam is a certified CTE Carpentry
teacher; Katelyn is a certified K-8 teacher.

*Tagline: "Human teaching. Life-changing tools."*

## Non-negotiables

- **No fabricated research.** Every citation is verified against the live source
  before it ships.
- **No student personal information** — anywhere, ever. The platform serves adult
  educators only.
- **Not legal advice.** Policy and FERPA content is informational, written by
  educators, and says so.
- **Certificates are Certificates of Completion** — never implied CEUs, clock hours,
  or license-renewal credit.
- One locked brand (Chalk Navy `#13293D` / Spark Teal `#2A9D8F` / Pencil Amber
  `#F4A825` / Paper `#F7F5F0` / Ink `#1B1F24` / Mist `#DCE3EA`, Inter type) across
  every page, deck, PDF, certificate, and email.
