Tips for using these cards

  • Laminate the set once and reorder with dry-erase or magnets all year — cut it once, use it all year.
  • Let your kids move the cards themselves. A child who arranges their own day owns the schedule instead of just receiving it.
  • Keep a blank "free card" in the deck for the days that need something the set doesn't name — a sick day, a field trip, a slow morning.
  • Re-cut a fresh set each semester as your rhythm changes — five minutes with scissors beats redesigning from scratch.
