Classroom procedures checklist

Every classroom runs on about twenty small procedures nobody teaches on purpose — until the year one teacher does. Check off each as you explicitly teach, model, and practice it. Add the date so you know when it's time to re-teach.

Entering & starting

How to enter the room and where to go first
Where to find and start the warm-up / bell-ringer
Where to put homework, notes, and permission slips
What to do if you're early / what to do if you're late

During the lesson

How to ask a question without raising every hand
Signal for quiet / signal for attention
How to get materials, sharpen a pencil, get a tissue
Bathroom / water procedure
What "early finisher" work looks like
How group work is formed and how it starts

Transitions & movement

Lining up (order, noise level, hands)
Moving between activities or centers
Fire drill / lockdown / emergency routes
Leaving for specials, lunch, or recess

Turning in & wrapping up

Where finished work goes, by subject
What a missing or late assignment looks like to you
End-of-day / end-of-class cleanup routine
Dismissal procedure (backpack, chairs, check-out)
date taught

Turn any row into poster wording, with AI

The routines-to-poster prompt

"Turn these classroom procedures into short, positive, poster-ready wording for [GRADE]: [PASTE THE ROWS YOU CHECKED]. Each one under 8 words, starting with a verb, no 'don't' statements."

Nothing about a specific student ever needs to go into this prompt — it's about the room, not a child.

Keep it visible, keep it current

  • Post the poster version where the class can see it — the AI prompt above turns any row into wall-ready wording in seconds.
  • Revisit after every break. Routines fade over a long weekend, and they evaporate over winter or spring break — plan five minutes to re-teach, not re-explain from scratch.
  • Update the "date taught" column whenever you re-teach a row — it becomes a quick record of what actually got reinforced this year.
