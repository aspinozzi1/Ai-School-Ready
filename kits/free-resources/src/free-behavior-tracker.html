Weekly summary & what it means

Add up the week, then match your total against the bands below for a fast read — no chart, no login required.

Needs a plan (below 30) Watching it (30–44) Solid week (45–60)

A total below 30 is worth bringing to a colleague, a coach, or a family conversation this week rather than waiting it out. Thresholds are a starting guide — trust your own read of the student over any number on this page.

If it's a low week

  • Look for a pattern before you look for a plan — same period each day, same subject, same seat neighbor. The tally column often shows it before the point total does.
  • Bring the paper, not just the memory, to whoever you loop in — a colleague, a coach, or the family. Numbers travel better than "it's been a rough week."
  • Keep rating the good days too. A tracker that only comes out for bad weeks stops being data and starts being a warning label.
