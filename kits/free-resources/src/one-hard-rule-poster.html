The One Hard Rule
Never put student personally identifiable information into a public AI tool.
And a human reviews everything before it reaches a student or family.
The ten-second test
Before you press enter, read your prompt once more and ask: could anyone identify this child from what I typed? If yes: strip the identity, generalize, and describe the need, not the child.
