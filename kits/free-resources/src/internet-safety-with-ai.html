Internet safety with AI: the five rules
1
A grown-up is always in the loop. Kids don't sign up for or chat alone with an AI tool — an adult sets it up and stays nearby.
2
Never type your real info. No full name, school, address, or photo — to an AI chatbot, a game, or a website.
3
AI can be confidently wrong. Check anything important somewhere else before you believe it or repeat it.
4
If it feels weird, tell a grown-up. Not in trouble — just telling. That's what keeps the internet safe for kids.
5
Strangers online stay strangers. A friendly chatbot or a friendly stranger — same rule: don't share, don't meet up.

Talking points for each rule

Post the rules, then talk through them once out loud — a ninety-second conversation does more than a silent poster. Here's a line for each rule that holds up to a kid's follow-up question.

  • 1 · A grown-up is in the loop. "AI chatbots are built for people 13 and up — that's their rule, not just ours. Below that, we explore it together."
  • 2 · Never type your real info. "The AI doesn't need your name to help — it only needs to know what you're asking about."
  • 3 · AI can be confidently wrong. "It guesses the next likely word — it doesn't actually know things the way a book or a teacher does. Let's check."
  • 4 · Tell a grown-up. "You won't be in trouble for telling me something weird happened. You'll only ever be in trouble for hiding it."
  • 5 · Strangers stay strangers. "A chatbot that sounds friendly is still not a person you know — same rules as anyone else online."

Where this fits

Pair it with our free My Digital Footprint mini-lesson for a full class period, or send the poster home as a family conversation-starter — either works.

Make it stick

  • Post it where devices live — near the classroom Chromebook cart, or the family tablet charger at home.
  • Revisit when something changes — a new AI tool at school, a new app a classmate mentions, a birthday near 13.
  • Practice rule 4 out loud once, so "tell a grown-up" is a rehearsed move, not a cold decision in the moment.
