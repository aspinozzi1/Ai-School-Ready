For the teacher — keep this page, don't send it home

Three fast ways to use these answers

  • Skim for icebreaker pairs. Two kids who both wrote "excited about" the same thing make an instant partner activity for week one.
  • Flag one follow-up per class, not per family. If three families mention the same worry (a new schedule, a move, a sibling starting school too), that's a five-minute announcement that answers it for everyone at once.
  • File the "reach me" info where you'll actually use it — your gradebook or contact sheet, the same day, before the stack grows.

Aggregate patterns across the whole stack are fine to think through with AI — "what are common first-week worries families mention" — but keep individual answers, and any names, out of the prompt.

Before you print

  • Print pages 1–2 for families — that's the whole form, front and back.
  • Keep this page for yourself. It never needs to leave your desk.
  • Send it home the first week and set a soft return date — a Friday, not a specific day one, works well.
