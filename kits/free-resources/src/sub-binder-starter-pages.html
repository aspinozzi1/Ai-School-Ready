The cover page

Substitute Teacher Binder
Room _____ · Teacher: ______________
Everything you need for today is inside this binder. Thank you for being here — start with Section 1.

Print, slide into a binder's clear front cover pocket, done.

Five dividers to print and tab

Section 1
Daily Schedule
Bell times, where the sub goes each period, duty coverage.
Section 2
Rosters & Seating
Class lists and a seating chart per class — printed, never emailed.
Section 3
Emergency Procedures
Fire, lockdown, evacuation map, nurse and office numbers.
Section 4
Ready-to-Grab Activity
One no-copier, no-login activity per prep, for the day everything else fails.
Section 5
Class Routines & Culture
Entering, dismissal, signals, and the "how this room runs" summary.

What to gather before you print

Current class rosters, one per period or block
Seating charts, printed and current — names live here, on paper
This week's bell schedule and any duty assignment
Front office and nurse extensions, plus your grade-level lead's room number
Two trusted student helpers per class you teach
One evergreen activity per prep, printed, no technology required

Print & assembly tips

  • Card stock for the dividers if you have it — they'll survive a whole year of pulling in and out of a binder.
  • Clear sheet protectors for the emergency procedures page and the contacts sheet — the two pages most likely to get grabbed in a hurry.
  • One binder per prep, or one per room — whichever matches how your building actually assigns substitutes.
  • Tell your front office where it lives. A binder nobody can find at 5:30 a.m. isn't a system yet.
