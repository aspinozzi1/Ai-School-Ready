# Parent Message Makeovers — provenance

- **Product**: Parent Message Makeovers ($12) — 12 family-communication scenarios,
  each with a ready-to-run, PII-safe AI prompt; the 4-part tone formula;
  build-your-own template; read-aloud pre-send checklist.
- **Built**: 2026-08-24 (Week 1 Monday batch, Back-to-School theme).
- **Source**: `src/Parent-Message-Makeovers.html` → PDF via
  `kits/tooling/build_pdf.js` (brand.css, Letter).
- **Method**: drafted with AI assistance, the same way we teach educators to use
  it, then researched, revised, and approved by two certified teachers.
- **Safety posture**: every prompt describes situations generally — no student
  names, no pasted parent emails, personalization happens in the teacher's own
  email after drafting. Scenario 6 uses actions-only incident language;
  scenario 9 explicitly avoids accusation and does not rely on AI detectors.
- **Claims audit**: page count and scenario count on cover/listing must match
  the built PDF (12 scenarios; check page total after every rebuild).
