Put the toolkit to work

PD that isn't used within two days mostly evaporates. Each action below uses something from today. Do all three, check the boxes, done.

ACTION 1

Teach with your lab artifact  ~10 min extra

You built a core version, a support version, and an extension for a real lesson. Teach it as planned, then take two minutes after class to jot one line: what did the support kids and the stretch kids actually do differently?

  • I taught with it  ·  What I noticed:
ACTION 2

Level one text, then check the level  ~10 min

Pick one passage from next week and run the "Level it" prompt from your handout. Then the habit: read a paragraph of the easier version aloud, eyeball the sentence lengths, and fix what the label got wrong. Check the level; don't trust the label.

  • I leveled and verified a text  ·  The label was: right / off (circle one)
ACTION 3

Post your best prompt to the staff doc  ~2 min

Whichever move worked best in the lab (level, scaffold, stretch, or reformat), paste your version into the staff prompt doc with your name and one line about the lesson it fit. Then borrow a colleague's differentiation prompt while you're in there.

  • My prompt is in the staff doc, and I borrowed one idea from someone else's
