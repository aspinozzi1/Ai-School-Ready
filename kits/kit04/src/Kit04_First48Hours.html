Three actions · each under 15 minutes · within two days

Put today's materials in front of real students

PD that isn't used within two days mostly evaporates. Each action below finishes something you started in the lab. Do all three, check the boxes, done.

ACTION 1

Hand the rubric to students  ~15 min

Give your lab rubric one last hand-it-to-a-student pass, attach it to the real assignment it was built for, and put it in students' hands when you assign the work. A rubric students read before they work is the whole point of the research.

  • My rubric went out with the assignment  ·  The assessment:
ACTION 2

Finish the vetting pass  ~10 min

Read every remaining question in your bank against the answer key. Fix or delete anything wrong, ambiguous, or off-level, then file the bank where you'll actually find it at quiz time. Drafts are cheap; vetting is sacred.

  • Every question checked  ·  Weak ones fixed or deleted:
ACTION 3

Use one feedback starter for real  ~15 min

On your next grading stack, use one of your three starters wherever its pattern shows up, and personalize every single one before it reaches a student. Then post your best starter to the staff doc's Assessment section if you didn't in the lab.

  • I used a starter on a real stack, personalized every time  ·  The pattern:

No student names in. No feedback out without your eyes on it. AI never grades.
