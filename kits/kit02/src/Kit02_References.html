References

APA style. The note beneath each entry says exactly where the kit uses it, so any claim can be traced to its source.

AI for Education. (n.d.). The Five "S" model: A prompt framework for educators [Resource]. https://www.aiforeducation.io/ai-resources/the-five-s-model Used for: evidence that teacher-specific prompting frameworks are established practice (facilitator background; slide 6 convergence claim).

Anthropic. (n.d.). Prompt engineering: Be clear, direct, and detailed [Vendor documentation]. https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/be-clear-direct Used for: the "colleague test" and the treat-it-like-a-brilliant-new-employee framing (slide 5; handout).

Bsharat, S. M., Myrzakhan, A., & Shen, Z. (2024). Principled instructions are all you need for questioning LLaMA-1/2, GPT-3.5/4 (arXiv:2312.16171) [Preprint; not peer-reviewed]. https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.16171 Used for: 26 prompt-design principles raised GPT-4 response-quality scores by 57.7% on the authors' own ATLAS benchmark (slide 7; admin one-pager). Cited as a preprint, and scoped in the text as a benchmark result rather than a general effect of prompting.

Gallup, & Walton Family Foundation. (2025). Teaching for tomorrow: Unlocking six weeks a year with AI. https://news.gallup.com/poll/691967/three-teachers-weekly-saving-six-weeks-year.aspx Used for: ~30% of teachers use AI weekly; weekly users save ~5.9 hours/week; 40% don't use AI at all (slide 2; prep guide; admin one-pager).

Google. (2024). Gemini for Google Workspace: Prompting guide 101 [Vendor guide]. https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/gemini_for_workspace_prompt_guide_october_2024_digital_final.pdf Used for: the persona/task/context/format elements underlying the kit's four-part formula (slide 6).

Kaufman, J. H., Woo, A., Eagan, J., Lee, S., & Kassan, E. B. (2025). Uneven adoption of artificial intelligence tools among U.S. teachers and principals in the 2023–2024 school year (RR-A134-25). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA134-25.html Used for: adoption is uneven across subjects and school poverty levels; skill instruction is an equity lever (facilitator background).

North Carolina Department of Public Instruction. (2024). North Carolina generative AI implementation recommendations and considerations for PK-13 public schools [State guidance]. https://www.dpi.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2024/01/16/ncdpi-releases-guidance-use-artificial-intelligence-schools Used for: a state DOE teaching a structured prompting framework (CRAFT), evidence the formula reflects mainstream K-12 guidance (slide 6).

OpenAI. (n.d.). Prompt engineering [Vendor documentation]. https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/prompt-engineering Used for: official strategies (clear instructions, reference text, task splitting) converging with the kit's formula (slide 6).

White, J., Fu, Q., Hays, S., Sandborn, M., Olea, C., Gilbert, H., Elnashar, A., Spencer-Smith, J., & Schmidt, D. C. (2023). A prompt pattern catalog to enhance prompt engineering with ChatGPT. Proceedings of the 30th Conference on Pattern Languages of Programs (PLoP). https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.11382 Used for: prompting as a learnable skill with documented, reusable patterns; the basis for the kit's template habit (slides 14 and 23).

Further reading: five worth your time

  • OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google prompting docs (linked above): short, free, and updated as models change; the primary sources for prompt technique.
  • AI for Education prompt library: ready-made educator prompts to adapt into your school prompt library. aiforeducation.io
  • Your state DOE's AI guidance: at least 28 states have published K-12 AI guidance; several include prompting frameworks. Find yours via the AI for Education state tracker.
  • Gallup & Walton Family Foundation, Teaching for Tomorrow series: the ongoing survey wave behind the six-weeks-a-year figure. waltonfamilyfoundation.org
  • White et al.'s prompt pattern catalog (linked above): for the colleague who wants the deep end; patterns map neatly onto classroom tasks.
