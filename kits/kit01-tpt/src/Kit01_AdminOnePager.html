AI Foundations & Safety: The One Hard Rule

A 45–60 minute, self-facilitated session that gives your whole staff a shared, safe, realistic foundation for AI, run by your own coach or teacher-leader, no consultant required.

Why this matters now

Teacher AI use roughly doubled in a single year: from about 25% in 2023–24 to more than half of teachers in 2024–25 (RAND). Over half of teens already use AI for schoolwork (Pew Research Center). Meanwhile RAND's own headline finding is that guidance lags adoption: the tools arrived before the training did. Your staff are already deciding, individually and invisibly, what goes into public AI tools. This session replaces those individual guesses with one clear, shared standard.

What your staff will be able to do afterward

  • State and apply the One Hard Rule: no student PII in public AI tools, ever
  • De-identify any prompt in ~10 seconds (strip · generalize · describe the need)
  • Use AI for a real task; every participant types during the session
  • Verify AI output rather than trust it (they'll know why it fabricates)
  • Apply the review rule: nothing AI-drafted reaches a student or family unread
  • Operate under three written staff commitments, your interim stance until formal policy
