Three actions · each under 15 minutes · within two days

Send what you already wrote

You did not leave the session with notes about family communication. You left with five finished pieces of writing. All three actions below are mostly send buttons.

ACTION 1

Send the four notes  ~10 min

They are written, each one carrying the true sentence you wrote in the lab. Paste them into your own email, one family at a time, and let them go. Do not add anything. Do not polish. The specific sentence is already the whole point.

  • Four sent  ·  Any reply that surprised you:
ACTION 2

Send the hard message  ~10 min

Read it out loud once before it goes; your ear catches blame faster than your eye. Check the four rules: the fact is first, there is exactly one ask with a date, you promised nothing you cannot deliver, and it is short enough that it did not need to be a phone call. Run the tone second read from your handout if you want another pair of eyes, then send it.

  • Sent  ·  Or called instead, because it needed a voice  ·  Date:
ACTION 3

Calendar next week's ten minutes  ~2 min

A recurring block, on a day with a name, ten minutes. Write the next four families in the note field now, while you still remember who has not heard from you. This is the action people skip and it is the one the whole habit depends on.

  • My ten minutes lives on:  ·  Next four families (initials):
