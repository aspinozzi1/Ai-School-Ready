Make the culture official

The lab drafted the culture; these three actions install it. The first two belong to the facilitator and leadership; the third belongs to everyone.

ACTION 1

The norms page goes out  Facilitator · ~30 min

Merge the group drafts into one page, five lines, in the building's words. Send it to the whole faculty for signing, and post the signed page where you'd post something you mean. The wording arguments were the culture forming; the signatures are it arriving.

ACTION 2

The first sharing round gets its date  Leadership · ~5 min

Win-fail-steal, five minutes, on the real calendar, with the owner the lab named. Leadership protects the five minutes out loud: that sentence, said publicly, is the cheapest culture investment available this month.

  • Calendared  ·  Date:  ·  Owner:
ACTION 3

Celebrate the certificate  Everyone · ~10 min

Track A is complete: eight sessions, eight sets of artifacts, one faculty. Announce the AI-Ready Educator Certificate of Completion the way this building celebrates real accomplishments: staff meeting, newsletter, the board report. Eight weeks of work deserves its moment; take it.
