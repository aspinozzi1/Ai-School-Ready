30-Day Implementation & Follow-Up Plan

The capstone drafted the culture; this month installs it. The routine runs twice, the norms get signed, the first artifacts of permanence appear, and leadership makes its three small promises in public.

Week by week

WeekWhat happensOwner
Week 1 The merged norms page circulates and gets signed; the signed page is posted. The first sharing round is calendared with its named owner. The certificate completion is celebrated publicly. Facilitator · Admin
Week 2 Sharing round #1 runs (win · fail · steal, 5 min). Leadership says the three promises out loud: the five minutes are protected, recovered hours won't be refilled, and nobody's individual AI use gets audited. Round owner · Admin
Week 3 Onboarding dry run: the named colleague walks one volunteer "new hire" through the welcome folder (norms page + library tour + Kit 1 date). Gaps get fixed while they're cheap. Onboarding owner
Week 4 Sharing round #2 runs. Leadership reviews the month against the four signals below and sets next semester's refresher calendar (one kit a month, from the 30-day plans). Round owner · Admin
The three promises leadership makes in public
The five minutes are protected. Recovered hours are not refilled. Individual AI use is never audited. Small enough to grant on the spot; public enough to be remembered.

The two sharing rounds, scripted

Round #1 · "Win · Fail · Steal"  Week 2 · 5 min

Win (2 min): one AI-drafted artifact that worked, shown or told. Fail (2 min): one thing the tool got wrong, shared without embarrassment; the room that laughs at hallucinated citations is the room that catches them. Steal (1 min): one template someone commits to taking from the library this week.

The owner's only job is protecting the clock and going first once. After two rounds, it runs itself.

Round #2 · same shape  Week 4 · 5 min

Same three beats. The only addition: the owner asks "what should next month's refresher be?" and the loudest answer becomes the first entry on next semester's calendar.

Leadership checklist for the month

  • Week 1: sign the norms page first, visibly; celebrate the certificate the way the building celebrates real things
  • Week 2: say the three promises out loud at a full-staff moment
  • Week 3: confirm the onboarding folder exists and has an owner
  • Week 4: collect one story for the board: the signed norms page, the library count, or a sharing-round moment; Track A completion is a board-report item

If the month goes sideways

SymptomFix
The norms page stalls unsignedIt's almost always wording, not resistance. Re-open the two contested lines at a sharing round and let the faculty fix the words; ownership returns with the pen.
The five minutes get cannibalizedThat's the promise leadership made in week 2; the fix is keeping it, visibly, at the very next meeting. Culture watches what survives a busy week.
Someone proposes monitoring teachers' AI useRe-anchor to the no-surveillance line: count artifacts, never people. Kit 5 taught the school what policing does to honesty; it applies to the adults too.
Energy dips after the capstone glowNormal. The refresher calendar exists for exactly this; run the most-requested kit's 10-minute follow-up and let the routine carry the rest.
