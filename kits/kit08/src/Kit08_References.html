References

APA style. The note beneath each entry says exactly where the kit uses it. As the capstone, this kit reuses sources verified for earlier kits' logs; each was re-confirmed for this use.

AI for Education. (2025). State AI guidance for education [tracker]. https://www.aiforeducation.io/ai-resources/state-ai-guidance Used for: 34 states and Puerto Rico have published K-12 AI guidance or policy (tracker updated 10/28/2025; checked August 2026). The school-level gap on slide 3 is attributed to RAND, not to this tracker, which measures states only.

Darling-Hammond, L., Hyler, M. E., & Gardner, M. (2017). Effective teacher professional development. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/effective-teacher-professional-development-report Used for: effective PD shares sustained duration, collaboration, active learning, and time to reflect, across 35 rigorous studies. That PD without them fades is AI-Ready School's design inference, not a tested finding in this report. The fade risk this capstone answers (slide 3; prep guide; admin one-pager).

Doss, C. J., et al. (2025). AI use in schools is quickly increasing but guidance lags behind: Findings from the RAND survey panels (RR-A4180-1). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA4180-1.html Used for: adoption outpaces written guidance in most schools (slide 3; prep guide; admin one-pager).

Gallup, & Walton Family Foundation. (2025). Teaching for tomorrow. https://news.gallup.com/poll/691967/three-teachers-weekly-saving-six-weeks-year.aspx Used for: majority adoption and the ~5.9 hours/week that the culture protects (slide 2 recap context).

Further reading: four worth your time

  • Darling-Hammond et al. (linked above): the seven elements of PD that works; this series was built against that list on purpose.
  • RAND's guidance-lag report (linked above): why a written stance puts your school ahead of most.
  • The state guidance tracker (linked above): what your state has published, for the day leadership formalizes policy (Kit 15).
  • Gallup & Walton's Teaching for Tomorrow (linked above): the adoption and time-savings landscape your culture now sits inside.
Our citation promise: every source in this kit was verified against the live publication, existence and claim, before inclusion. Where full author lists could not be verified, "et al." is used rather than guessed names. If a link moves, tell us and we'll ship the fix to every member school.
