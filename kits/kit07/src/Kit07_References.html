References

APA style. The note beneath each entry says exactly where the kit uses it, so any claim can be traced to its source.

Darling-Hammond, L., Hyler, M. E., & Gardner, M. (2017). Effective teacher professional development. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/effective-teacher-professional-development-report Used for: effective PD requires active learning and sustained follow-up; the basis for the lab + 30-day design (prep guide).

Doss, C. J., et al. (2025). AI use in schools is quickly increasing but guidance lags behind (RR-A4180-1). RAND Corporation. https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA4180-1.html Used for: majority teacher AI adoption context (prep guide; admin one-pager).

Gallup. (2022). K-12 workers have highest burnout rate in U.S. https://news.gallup.com/poll/393500/workers-highest-burnout-rate.aspx Used for: in Gallup's February 3-14, 2022 workforce survey, 44% of K-12 workers and 52% of teachers reported feeling burned out "always" or "very often," the highest of the industry, vs. 30% of all other workers (slide 2; slide 17; prep guide; admin one-pager).

Gallup, & Walton Family Foundation. (2025). Teaching for tomorrow. https://news.gallup.com/poll/691967/three-teachers-weekly-saving-six-weeks-year.aspx Used for: weekly AI users save ~5.9 hours/week, about six weeks per school year (slides 3 and 25; prep guide; admin one-pager).

Merrimack College, & EdWeek Research Center. (2022). How teachers spend their time: A breakdown. Education Week. https://www.edweek.org/teaching-learning/how-teachers-spend-their-time-a-breakdown/2022/04 Used for: the ~54-hour work week with under half spent directly teaching (slide 2; prep guide; admin one-pager).

Further reading: four worth your time

  • Gallup's burnout poll (linked above): the industry comparison that should be on every school board's desk.
  • Gallup & Walton's Teaching for Tomorrow (linked above): where the recoverable hours actually are, by task.
  • Merrimack/EdWeek's time study (linked above): the anatomy of the 54-hour week.
  • Darling-Hammond et al. (linked above): why one-and-done PD fails and follow-ups work; the design behind this whole series.
Our citation promise: every source in this kit was verified against the live publication, existence and claim, before inclusion. Where full author lists could not be verified, "et al." is used rather than guessed names. If a link moves, tell us and we'll ship the fix to every member school.
