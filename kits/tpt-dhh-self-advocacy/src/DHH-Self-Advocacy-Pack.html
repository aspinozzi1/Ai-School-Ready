Self-Advocacy Worksheets for Deaf & Hard of Hearing Students

Four short, reusable worksheets a Teacher of the Deaf, itinerant ToD, or case manager works through with a student — upper-elementary through high school. Wording is written to flex across that range; adapt it further to your student's grade and reading level as needed.

Before you begin
This is a teaching support, not a medical or device recommendation.
However a student communicates — ASL, spoken language, a hearing device, or a combination — these pages adapt to that student, not the other way around.

This week: check what's true for you

This isn't your IEP — it's a quick way to notice what's working right now and what's worth bringing up at your next check-in. Check off anything that's true for you this week.

Student self-check (checkboxes)
