Three actions · each under 15 minutes · within two days

Put your game in front of a class

PD that is not used within two days mostly evaporates. Each action below uses the game you built today. Do all three, check the boxes, done.

ACTION 1

Play it with one class, then change one thing  ~15 min

Run your game with one class, or a colleague standing in. Watch what confuses them. Then change exactly one thing by describing it, using the boundary sentence, and play it again to check. Save a copy first.

  • I played it, and I changed one thing  ·  The change:
ACTION 2

Fix the first real bug  ~10 min

There will be one, and that is normal. A blank page, a typo in an answer, a change that touched the wrong thing. Use the recovery lines from the handout: read the error to the AI, or reopen your saved copy and ask again, narrower.

  • I found a bug and fixed it  ·  What it was:
ACTION 3

Send your file to one colleague  ~5 min

Email or share the saved game file itself with one teacher. Ask them to open it and tell you one thing they would change. Share the file, not the story. Commitment #4: the game gets played with real students by Friday.
