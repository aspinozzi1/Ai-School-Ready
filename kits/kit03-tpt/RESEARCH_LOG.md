## What changed from Tuesday's brief, and why

The brief (`tpt/intel/2026-09-23-wed-brief.md`) flagged all seven of its own rows as
unverified web research from 2026-09-21 and required re-verification before any figure
reached a slide. Re-verification (a dedicated research pass, 2026-09-23) found:

- **"More than 70 bills across 27 states" corrected to the exact figure, 77 bills, 27
  states** (source 5). The brief's rounded phrasing was directionally right but not
  precise enough for a paid product; the deck and script both now print "77 bills, 27
  states."
- **Maryland's July 1, 2027 date is the law's own stated intent for a train-the-trainer
  model, not an absolute compliance deadline with a penalty attached.** The deck's
  slide 2 notes say "with the state's own goal of finishing by July 1, 2027" rather than
  presenting it as a hard mandate deadline, matching the statute's actual language
  ("with the intent of training educators by July 1, 2027").
- **Ohio is cited only for its AI-policy-adoption mandate, not as a training mandate
  "comparable" to Maryland's.** The original web research risked overstating Ohio's law;
  Ohio's HB 96 requires a policy by July 1, 2026, and does not carry a statewide teacher
  PD requirement on the same footing as Maryland's SB 720. The script's stage direction
  on slide 2 flags this distinction explicitly for the facilitator.
- **"Most districts remain underprepared" (an unattributed, unsourced phrase in the
  brief) was replaced with two dated, numbered, sourced findings**: Gallup & Walton's
  82%-no-formal-guidance figure and IBM/Morning Consult's 20%-extensive-training /
  42%-cite-training-as-top-barrier figures (sources 6-7). Both are 2026 publications,
  both are named and cited on slide 4 and in the facilitator script.
- **Virginia's compensation/PD provisions verified independently of Maryland's** (source
  3): Virginia's laws direct PD as part of state guidance, distinct from and consistent
  with Maryland's stronger, explicit compensation clause (source 2), which is the one
  quoted on slide 3.

**Claims deliberately avoided (could not verify to this kit's standard):** any specific
dollar figure for Virginia's pilot program beyond the $2M total (per-district amounts
were not confirmed); any claim that Ohio's model policy (due from ODEW by Dec. 31, 2025)
has been published or reviewed, since that fell outside this verification pass; any
suggestion that the 77-bill FutureEd count and the competing MultiState tracker's count
(134 bills, 31 states, a broader definition) are the same measurement — only FutureEd's
number and methodology are cited, by name, on the slide.
