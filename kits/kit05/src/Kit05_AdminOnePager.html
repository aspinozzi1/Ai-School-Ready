AI & Academic Integrity: Setting Clear Expectations with Students

A 45–60 minute session in which every teacher writes the AI rules for a real upcoming assignment, in student language, and practices a fair process for suspected misuse. Nothing here asks you to adopt a policy: teachers start in their own rooms, and you decide later whether it is worth making consistent. Run by your own coach; no consultant required.

The stance, and your parent answer
Clarity beats policing. Every assignment states its AI rules in writing, honesty is taught like a skill, and no student is ever accused based on an AI-detector score alone.

Why this matters now

54% of teens use AI for schoolwork (Pew, 2026) while guidance lags adoption (RAND, 2025), so most students have had little explicit guidance. Stanford's surveys found self-reported cheating flat at 60–70% across ChatGPT's arrival (Lee, Pope et al., 2024): clarity, not panic, is the fix. The detector route carries real risk: OpenAI shut down its own detector for low accuracy, and commercial detectors flagged over half of real essays by non-native English speakers as AI-generated (Liang et al., 2023).

What your staff will be able to do afterward

  • Label every assignment with one of three AI lanes: AI-free, AI-assisted, or AI-included
  • Print the school's standard AI Box on assignments, in language students can quote back
  • Use a ready-made AI Disclosure block (fixed checkboxes, nothing to draft) that makes honest AI use visible and coachable
  • Design assignments where the thinking is visible: drafts, local anchors, live defenses
  • Run a curiosity-first conversation for suspected misuse, built on process evidence
  • State the red line cold: no accusation on a detector score alone, ever

Research base in brief

Cheating rates stable pre/post ChatGPT: Lee, Pope, Miles, & Zárate (2024) · Teen AI use: Pew Research Center (2026) · Adoption vs. guidance gap: RAND RR-A4180-1 (2025) · Detector bias against non-native writers: Liang et al., Patterns (2023). Full APA citations ship in the kit's References file.
