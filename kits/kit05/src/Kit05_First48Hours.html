Three actions · each under 15 minutes · within two days

Put the rules where students can see them

PD that isn't used within two days mostly evaporates. Each action below finishes something you started in the lab. Do all three, check the boxes, done.

ACTION 1

Hand out the labeled assignment  ~10 min

Put your lane label and filled-in AI Box on the assignment you built for in the lab, and hand it out. Read the box aloud when you assign the work; sixty seconds of "here's what's okay on this one" is the whole rollout.

  • The box went out with the assignment  ·  Its lane:
ACTION 2

Teach the Disclosure block once  ~5 min

Pick one class and teach the two-line disclosure: what tool I used, what it did for me. Show an example, say the soft-landing rule out loud ("if you tell me, we talk; nobody gets ambushed for honesty"), and answer the first gray-area question that comes up.

  • One class knows the norm  ·  Their first gray-area question:
ACTION 3

File your two sentences  ~2 min

Put the two curiosity-first sentences you wrote in the lab somewhere you'll actually find them in a hard moment: taped inside your gradebook, pinned in your notes app. The calm version of you wrote them; make sure the frustrated version can find them.

  • My sentences are filed where I'll find them  ·  Where:
