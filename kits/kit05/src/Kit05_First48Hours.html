Three actions · each under 15 minutes · within two days

Hand out the sheet you just rebuilt

Each action below finishes something you started in the lab. Do all three, check the boxes, done.

ACTION 1

Hand it out, and read the AI line aloud  ~10 min

Give the assignment you rebuilt in the lab, with the added sentence and the one line about AI printed on it. When you assign it, read that line out loud. Sixty seconds of "here is what's okay on this one" is the entire rollout, and it is the part that keeps honest students from guessing wrong.

  • It went out  ·  The one thing I asked for:
ACTION 2

Pick assignment number two  ~10 min

Take the next assignment on your calendar and run the same four steps: what it's for, the one thing, the one line. The second one takes about half as long as the first, which is the whole reason this is a move and not a project. Stuck? That usually means places one and two are wrong for this task; use place three and put two minutes of it in front of you.

  • Assignment two is rebuilt  ·  Which place I used:
ACTION 3

File the opening line  ~2 min

Write "Walk me through how you made this. What did you start with?" somewhere the tired version of you will find it: taped inside your gradebook, pinned in your notes app. The calm version of you is filing it now so the frustrated version does not have to improvise.

  • Filed where I'll find it  ·  Where:
