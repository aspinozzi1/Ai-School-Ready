Prompting Basics: Getting Useful Results Every Time

A 45–60 minute, self-facilitated session that turns AI access into AI skill, and leaves your school with a growing shared prompt library. Run by your own coach or teacher-leader; no consultant required.

Why this matters now

The 2025 Gallup survey of 2,232 teachers found that weekly AI users save about 5.9 hours a week, roughly six weeks a year, while 40% of teachers don't use AI at all. Access is identical; skill is not. Prompting is the learnable skill that closes that gap, and researchers who tested structured prompts against casual ones measured response-quality gains of about 58% on their own benchmark. This session teaches the skill in one hour and installs the habit system that keeps it growing.

What your staff will be able to do afterward

  • Write four-part prompts (role · task · context · format) instead of vague requests
  • Apply the "colleague test" before submitting any prompt
  • Iterate deliberately: content, voice, details, instead of settling for first drafts
  • Own one reusable prompt template for a weekly task, built during the session
  • Contribute to a shared staff prompt library that compounds all year
  • State what prompting can't fix: verification and judgment stay human
