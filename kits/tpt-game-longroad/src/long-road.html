One question per line, eight parts, separated by the | character.

      
question | correct answer | wrong | wrong | wrong | idea | level | why

      
idea is the concept it tests. The game tracks mastery by idea, brings a
      missed idea back later, and never asks two of the same idea in a row, so use only three or four
      ideas in a round. level is 1 easy, 2 medium, 3 hard — students pick their own difficulty by
      picking the road, so you need some of each. why is the one line a student sees after getting
      it wrong, and it is the part that does the teaching.
